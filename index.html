Trust & Safety

Built around trust.

Peer-to-peer rentals work only when both sides trust the platform. SkipIt is being built with multiple layers of trust mechanisms designed for the Indian rental market.

Identity & KYC Verification

Users verify their identity before accessing the marketplace. KYC helps establish accountability and builds trust between renters and owners.

User Profiles & Reviews

Transparent profiles with ratings and reviews from past rentals. A track record that helps both renters and owners make informed decisions.

Listing Moderation

All listings go through automated checks before going live. Our system screens for prohibited items, suspicious content, and policy violations.

Fraud & Spam Detection

Automated systems monitor for suspicious activity, duplicate listings, abnormal pricing, and fraudulent behaviour across the platform.

Active monitoring

Secure Payments

Payments processed securely through the platform. Deposits and rental payments are handled with clear policies that protect both parties.

Dispute & Support System

A structured resolution process for disputes between renters and owners. Our support team is available to assist with rental issues.

Platform technology

More than listings. A smarter marketplace.

SkipIt builds marketplace intelligence into the platform — helping maintain listing quality, detect issues early, and support a healthier rental community.

Automated Listing Checks

Listings are reviewed against content policies before going live on the marketplace.

Duplicate Detection

System-level checks identify and flag duplicate or near-identical listings.

Spam & Keyword Screening

Suspicious keywords and patterns are flagged before listing publication.

Abnormal Pricing Detection

Pricing outliers — unusually high or suspiciously low — are flagged for review.

Image & Content Checks

Listing images and content are reviewed for quality, relevance, and policy compliance.

Fraud Indicators

Behavioural patterns associated with fraudulent activity are monitored and actioned.

Human Moderation

Listings flagged by automated systems are reviewed by our human moderation team.

Risk-Based Monitoring

Ongoing monitoring focuses attention on higher-risk transaction patterns and users.
